# Wisp privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

Wisp is an alarm clock app. It does not collect, store remotely, or share any personal data.

- **No data leaves your device.** Wisp does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
- **What is stored on your device:** your alarms, timers, world-clock cities and settings, kept in the app's private storage. Uninstalling Wisp, or clearing its data, deletes them.
- **Backups:** if you have Android backup enabled, Android may include Wisp's alarms and settings in your device backup, handled by Google under your backup settings. Wisp itself has no access to that backup.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.**
- **Permissions** are used only for alarm functions: notifications and full-screen alerts to ring, exact alarms to ring on time, vibration, keeping the device awake while ringing, and restarting alarms after a reboot.
