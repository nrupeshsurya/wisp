Most alarm lists fill up with one-off alarms you set once and never need again. Wisp fixes that: one-time alarms are temporary — after they ring, they quietly move to "Recently ended" (where you can restore them) and are cleared after 7 days. Your alarm list only shows what's still coming.

<b>Set alarms by typing</b>
Type what you mean and Wisp shows how it understood you before adding it:
• "nap 20m" or "in 45 min laundry"
• "7:15 gym tomorrow" or "fri 9am dentist"
• "weekdays 6:45 work" or "every mon and wed 7am yoga"

<b>Labels that matter</b>
The label is the biggest thing on the ring screen, so you know why you set the alarm the moment it goes off. Recent labels are one tap away.

<b>Everything a clock needs</b>
• Next-alarm card: "Next alarm in 7 h 12 min"
• Skip the next occurrence of a repeating alarm without turning it off
• Snooze, gradual volume, auto-silence, per-alarm sound and vibration
• Swipe to delete with undo
• Timers with keypad entry, a stopwatch with laps, and a world clock
• Works with assistants that set alarms and timers

<b>Reliable</b>
Wisp uses Android's alarm-clock API, so alarms ring on time even in battery-saving modes, wake the screen over the lock screen, and survive restarts. If the phone was off when an alarm was due, Wisp tells you instead of silently dropping it.

<b>Light and private</b>
About 1.5 MB, no ads, no accounts, no tracking. Wisp has no internet permission at all — your alarms never leave your phone.

Open source (GPL-3.0), based on Fossify Clock.
